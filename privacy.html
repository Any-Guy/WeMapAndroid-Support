Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

WeMap is built from the ground up to empower field surveyors, geologists, and GIS professionals while respecting user privacy. This policy details how your spatial data is secured locally on your device.

2. Data Collection & Processing

WeMap does not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information on external servers.

  • Location Data (GPS): Used strictly in real-time to compute waypoints, map current coordinates, log track vertices, and calculate live bearings. Location information is never transmitted to external telemetry servers.
  • Spatial Database & GIS Files: All imported shapefiles, GeoJSON, KML archives, and Room spatial database tables (map_layers and map_features) remain exclusively stored inside your device's sandboxed storage.
  • Attribute Schemas: Custom attributes, notes, and values entered in the Layer Schema Editor remain strictly on-device.

3. Data Sharing & Exports

WeMap has no cloud telemetry, third-party trackers, or ad integrations. Data only leaves your device when you explicitly choose to export or share your files (such as generating a Shapefile .zip, .geojson, .kml, or geotagged image).

4. Third‑Party Services

WeMap utilizes the Google Maps SDK to render base satellite and vector maps. Google may gather anonymized diagnostics in adherence with the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention & Device Security

Because all spatial layers and attributes are held within the Android Room database locally on your phone, device-level operating system encryption protects your projects. Clearing the app's cache or uninstalling WeMap completely wipes all saved layers, database tables, and local caches.

6. Your Controls

You maintain 100% control over your GIS project data at all times, with the ability to delete individual layers, clear attribute schemas, or export your work to open formats.
